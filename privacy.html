Privacy Policy

ClipForge (the Vice City Daily clipping tool) · Effective 20 November 2026

Who this covers

ClipForge is a desktop app used only by the owner of the Vice City Daily YouTube channel to publish and manage that channel's own videos. It is not offered to the public. This policy explains what it does with the Google and YouTube data it can access when the owner signs in with Google.

What ClipForge accesses

  • Channel details: the channel's ID, name and public statistics.
  • Uploading and managing videos (youtube.upload, youtube.force-ssl): uploading and scheduling the channel's own videos, changing their titles, and deleting or making private the channel's own scheduled uploads.
  • Reading the channel's videos and statistics (youtube.readonly): views, likes and comment counts of the channel's own videos.
  • YouTube Analytics (yt-analytics.readonly, yt-analytics-monetary.readonly): the channel's watch time, audience retention and estimated revenue.

ClipForge does not access Gmail, Google Drive, contacts or any data of other channels or viewers.

How the data is used

Only to publish the channel's videos and to show the owner how they perform (which posting times, lengths and styles work best). The data is never sold, never used for advertising, and never used by us to train AI models.

Where it is stored

On the owner's own computer: the Google sign-in token and the statistics are kept in ClipForge's local files and database. There is no ClipForge server.

Services that process it

To write performance summaries for the owner, ClipForge may send the channel's own aggregate numbers (views and retention per video) to AI assistants the owner uses: Anthropic's Claude and OpenAI's ChatGPT. They receive only what is needed for the summary, under those providers' own terms. Google's services (YouTube Data API and YouTube Analytics API) are used under the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements, and the YouTube Terms of Service.

Keeping and deleting data

Data stays on the owner's computer until the owner deletes it in ClipForge or removes the app. Access can be withdrawn at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions; ClipForge then can no longer read or change anything on the channel.
